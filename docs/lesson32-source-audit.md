## 6. Ambiguities / items flagged (NOT invented, NOT silently changed)

1. **§33 classification of `has studied robotics for three years` as Present Perfect.** The source classifies it as Present Perfect with the reason «نهتم بالمدة والتجربة المرتبطة بالحاضر». In the rest of this lesson, duration phrases are taught as PPC cues. The source is the authority, so the answer key keeps *has studied → Present Perfect* as written. A **teacher-only note** flags that both forms are used in English for this kind of duration/experience sentence, and that the lesson's own rule (§4–§7) would normally highlight the continuous form. The student is never asked to change this answer.
2. **§22 and §23 use hedges («عادةً»).** Stative-verb and "number" rules are taught as *usual* patterns, not absolute laws. Platform explanations keep the hedge.
3. **§18** says PPC does not necessarily mean the action is still continuing. Taught verbatim.
4. **§20** refers back to Lesson 31 («مثلما تعلمنا في الدرس 31»). Kept as source.
5. **§25–26** reference Lesson 29 (Past Perfect Continuous). Kept as source.
6. **§28 and §32** use bracketed cue verbs and time words that are sometimes ambiguous in isolation (e.g. «this week»). The source answers are kept, and the answer text is shown only after attempt.

No source line was found to be unreadable. No source content was invented.
