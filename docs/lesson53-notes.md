# Lesson 53 — implementation notes

- The lesson uses `Lesson1Shell` without modification. Its slide frame and
  presentation primitives are the existing Lesson 1-derived exports in
  `lesson52/kit52.tsx`; the cover/closing retain Lesson 1's classes. A local
  bottom margin on the scroll area keeps interactive controls clear of the
  floating site lock; no shared architectural file is changed.
- All 25 final-test prompts are student data; the key is imported only after
  submission. The static chunk and this public repository remain accessible:
  this is a presentation gate, not security. Some model practice answers also
  overlap naturally with test material, as in the supplied source.
- The text-answer checker accepts a selection of common variants. An unmatched
  free-text response is **not automatically declared wrong**: after submission
  it is flagged for learner/teacher review, which can adjust the provisional
  score. This is not automated semantic grading, and self-review depends on
  the reviewer. Choice questions are scored automatically.
- Exercise 2 question 1: **were** is the preferred formal/exam answer, but
  **was** is used informally and is explicitly recognized in feedback.
- Reading question 2's source answer says Adam dreams of London. The text says
  he dreams of a big city and mentions London hypothetically, not expressly as
  his dream. The source answer is retained and the ambiguity is noted when it
  is revealed. In exercise 4, items 1 and 5 can have odd meanings despite
  being structurally correct, so the originals are retained with a note.
- The source's writing example has eight sentences while the assignment asks
  for 10–14; both are retained, with the distinction explained to the learner.
- Lesson 54 is not registered in the current repository. The next-lesson link
  follows the existing hash convention and will lead to the hub until 54 is
  implemented; this is identified in the lesson.
